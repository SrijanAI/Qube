## Business Model (Current)
Two interdependent sides:

**Deal source** — find AI companies that need training data, get into their supply chain
**Execution source** — build and manage the contributor network that delivers the data

Both sides must grow together. A deal without execution capacity = failure. Execution capacity without deals = waste.

## Core Service: AI Training Data Collection
Regional human data collection for AI training:
- **Egocentric (POV) video data** — people recording daily home activities from head-mounted camera
- **Voice/speech data** — people speaking on different topics, multiple languages
- **Video data** — clothing/360 degree recordings, activity recognition
- **Target regions**: India (active), South America + Philippines (exploring), SEA + Japan (planned)

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
**Primary**: Early-to-mid stage AI companies (Seed–Series B, $5M–$150M raised) building physical, embodied, or voice AI — where their model needs to understand real human activity in real environments.

Best-fit project types (ranked):
1. Egocentric / first-person video AI (exact match to Atlas Capture)
2. Home robotics / embodied AI (need diverse household activity footage)
3. Wearable / AR AI (head-mounted camera context)
4. Voice/speech AI with India or SEA market
5. Healthcare AI with India deployment

Key signals a company is a buyer:
- "Training data" or "human data" in job listings or blog posts
- "Diverse" or "global" coverage mentioned as a challenge
- India or Asia listed as target market
- Recently funded (last 6 months) — hasn't locked a vendor yet
- No Scale AI / Appen partnership announced

NOT the ICP: pure SaaS AI, NLP/text-only, autonomous vehicles, large companies with Scale/Appen contracts, European companies with no India/Asia play.
