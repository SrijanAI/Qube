# Qube Agency - Key Decisions

## Aug 17, 2026 - Stop Web3 Cold Outreach
**Decision**: Stop all web3/crypto cold lead generation immediately.
**Reason**: 5 months of data show it doesn't convert. Only referral deals are closing.
**Impact**: Pranav + Aditya resources freed up → redirect to AI execution capacity building.

## Aug 17, 2026 - Referral-Only Model
**Decision**: Shift to referral-only deal flow for both web3 (pass to partners) and AI (execute in-house).
**Reason**: Only deals closing in last 2 months came from referrals, not cold outreach.

## Aug 23, 2026 - GMV Definition Problem Identified
**Decision**: Require deposit before deal counts as closed and before execution starts.
**Reason**: April-July shows GMV recorded but near-zero cash collected. Deals "locked" verbally but execution never starting.

## Sep 2026 - Pivot to Supply Chain Model
**Decision**: Shift from referral-only to active supply chain entry strategy.
**Reason**: Referral model is too passive and dependent on KGen ecosystem. Need independent deal channels.
**New model**: Two parallel tracks — agency partnerships (get into existing supply chains) + cold outreach (find buyers directly).

## Sep 2026 - Mid-Tier Agency Strategy
**Decision**: Focus agency outreach on mid-tier agencies (Taskmonk, Cogito, Cloudfactory) not Tier 1 (iMerit, Appen).
**Reason**: Low tier agencies rarely have deals. Tier 1 agencies need deep market knowledge before giving deals. Mid-tier have deals but just need trust — achievable by giving a deal first.
**Approach**: Share the KGen 5 lakh hours deal with agencies before asking for anything. Give first.

## Sep 2026 - KGen Dependency Identified as Risk
**Decision**: Build independent deal channels in parallel while delivering for KGen.
**Reason**: All 3 current deals (Humyn Labs, Atlas Capture, Decart) trace to one source. Single point of failure.
**Rule**: Do not alert KGen to direct outreach to Atlas Capture or other KGen clients.

## Sep 2026 - ICP Defined
**Decision**: Primary ICP is early-to-mid stage AI companies building physical, embodied, or voice AI — needing real human activity data from Indian/Asian environments.
**Reason**: Cold outreach was pulling off-target companies (film studios, translation tools, French doc companies). Needed to tighten.
**Action**: AI product teams is the only segment showing signal (1.2% reply rate). AI data managers to be paused until targeting fixed.

## Sep 2026 - Atlas Capture Execution Approach
**Decision**: Recruit 15-20 dedicated iPhone users, Qube lends headmounts (~₹16,000 total investment).
**Reason**: College network (~500 people) has very low iPhone penetration. Cannot hit 1-2k hour target without dedicated contributors.
**Owner**: Srijan to discuss with Soham/Pranav for sourcing.
